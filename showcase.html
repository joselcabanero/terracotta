Terracotta — a design system born from the heartbeat: pulse, rhythm, impact.

Foundations

Colour — structural, not decorative

Two mother colours anchor the system: EAV Red is the mark, EAV Cream is the editorial ground. Colour is assigned by hierarchy and meaning. Yellow is only the light point; orange is only a gradient stop; burgundy is depth.

Foundations

Typography — Rubik, one family

Headlines are Rubik Regular with an initial capital; emphasis is a single bold fragment, and at most one word may turn red. Paragraphs left-aligned, never justified. A system mono is kept only as a functional fallback.

Charts — colour by relevance
