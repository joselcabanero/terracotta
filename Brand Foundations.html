Operational clarity

We work with the corporate from challenge to the validated pilot.

Body text is Regular with bold highlights. In long documents it moves to Light with Medium highlights for a lighter, more balanced read. Paragraphs are left-aligned, never justified.

Graphic elements

Pulse, rhythm and the light point

The heartbeat in the form of light. The hero gradient is reserved for brand moments; product surfaces lean on the soft cream pause and the precise yellow light point.

Main brand gradient
Radial Yellow → Orange → Red → Burgundy. Covers, heroes and impact moments only.
Secondary cream
A clean, soft pause for landing pages and product surfaces dense with reds.
Light point
