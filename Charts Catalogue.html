Charts on the brand hierarchy

Every chart type uses the same fixed color order — assigned by relevance, never decoration. Rubik throughout, warm hairline axes, rounded ends, no 3D, no gridlines louder than the data. Seventeen types below, from KPI tiles to sankey and sunburst.

Radar

Multi-criteria profile

One profile in red with an 18% fill; the benchmark in grey behind. Max six axes, hex or pentagon rings, dashed inner guides.

Scatter / bubble

Correlation · two measures

Population in grey; the one or two points that matter in red and orange, sized by a third measure. Quadrant guides dashed.

Waterfall

Bridge from start to end

Totals in black and burgundy; increases in red; decreases hollow grey — reduction is neutral, not "bad". Dashed connectors.

Treemap

Hierarchical share

Cells ordered by size, colored by rank; white 3px gutters and 8px corners. Label + share inside; drop labels on cells too small to hold them.

Sankey

Flow between stages

Flows carry their source node's rank color at ~55% opacity; dropped/neutral flows go grey. The central stage is an EAV Black node. Never rainbow the links.

Color by relevance

Rank the series, then color in order: Red → Orange → Burgundy → Yellow → Dark Cream. Grey for benchmarks and context. Never assign colors by category meaning.

Type & axes

Rubik everywhere. Axis labels 11px in the warm muted grey; value labels 500 weight in EAV Black. Hairline axes, dashed guides — no boxed plots.

Restraint

Max five series or segments. No 3D, no shadows, no full pies. The only gradient inside a chart is the single-series area fade or a heatmap ramp.
